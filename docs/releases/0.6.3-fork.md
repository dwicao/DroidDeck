# DroidDeck 0.6.3 — Find plugged-in USB SSDs

Fixes USB SSDs and other USB OTG drives not appearing in DroidDeck at all: the drive list
no longer drops volumes that Android reports without a UUID, keeps drives that are plugged in
but not yet mounted, and falls back to reading kernel mounts, so a plugged-in drive shows up
even when Android does not expose it as a storage volume or an app folder. A drive that is
visible but not readable explains itself and points to the “All files access” system setting,
which DroidDeck needs to read and write USB drives and memory cards.

- `Setup > Storage > Games > Move` lists every readable removable drive, so a USB SSD can be
  picked directly without configuring it as Game storage first. The folder picker also starts
  on the first removable drive when no card is set.
- Removable drives keep their system names instead of all being called “SD card”.
- The 0.6.2 move-target listing and folder auto-creation, and the 0.6.1 move support, are
  unchanged: game files, Proton prefix, shader cache, workshop content, pending downloads, and
  manifest move together, the destination is registered as a Steam library, and a failed copy
  rolls back.

### Before you move

- Stop the Steam session first. The move runs in the app, not in Steam, and is not resumable: keep
  DroidDeck open and do not unplug the storage until it finishes.

### Install

- versionName `0.6.3`, versionCode `28`. It installs over the fork's 0.6.2 (versionCode `27`) and
  keeps app data.
- The APK is signed with the public AOSP test key. Android may show a Play Protect warning.
- Requires Android 9 or newer. A supported Adreno device is still required for the Steam session.

The APK and its SHA-256 are in the release assets.
