# DroidDeck 0.6.2 — Move games to any plugged-in drive

`Setup > Storage > Games > Move` now lists every attached removable volume as a target, so a
freshly plugged USB SSD or SD card can be picked even when it is not the configured Game storage
and does not have a `steam` folder yet. The folder is created on demand and writability is probed
with a real write instead of trusting the filesystem flag.

### Also in this build

- 0.6.1's move support: a Steam game and its Proton prefix, shader cache, workshop content,
  pending downloads, and manifest move into internal storage, the SD card, a configured games
  folder, or a picked folder. The destination is registered as a Steam library for the game's
  client (arm64 or x86-64), a failed copy rolls back, and stale legacy prefix and shader cache
  copies are removed after a successful move.
- The fork's default-storage picker, panel pacing and GPU clock pin, game-kit export/import, and
  x86 Linux runtime handling are unchanged.

### Before you move

- Stop the Steam session first. The move runs in the app, not in Steam, and is not resumable: keep
  DroidDeck open and do not unplug the storage until it finishes. If Android kills the app mid-move,
  some files can be left in the destination.

### Install

- versionName `0.6.2`, versionCode `27`. It installs over the fork's 0.6.1 (versionCode `26`) and
  keeps app data.
- The APK is signed with the public AOSP test key. Android may show a Play Protect warning.
- Requires Android 9 or newer. A supported Adreno device is still required for the Steam session.

The APK and its SHA-256 are in the release assets.
