# DroidDeck 0.6.4 — Plugged-in drives always listed

Fixes USB SSDs still missing from the drive list: volumes that report no UUID and no folder
are no longer dropped, plugged-in but unmounted or unsupported-filesystem volumes stay visible
instead of vanishing silently, app folders are only matched on strict UUIDs, and kernel mounts
are accepted under all vold-managed locations plus USB-ish mounts directly under `/mnt`.

- The folder picker and `Setup > Storage > Games > Move` now see the SSD even when Android
  does not expose it as a storage volume or an app folder. A drive that is visible but not
  readable explains itself and points to the “All files access” system setting, which DroidDeck
  needs to read and write USB drives and memory cards.
- 0.6.3's `/proc/mounts` fallback, system drive names, move-target listing, and folder
  auto-creation are unchanged, as are the 0.6.1/0.6.2 move, rollback, and library registration.

### Before you move

- Stop the Steam session first. The move runs in the app, not in Steam, and is not resumable: keep
  DroidDeck open and do not unplug the storage until it finishes.

### Install

- versionName `0.6.4`, versionCode `29`. It installs over the fork's 0.6.3 (versionCode `28`) and
  keeps app data.
- The APK is signed with the public AOSP test key. Android may show a Play Protect warning.
- Requires Android 9 or newer. A supported Adreno device is still required for the Steam session.

The APK and its SHA-256 are in the release assets.
